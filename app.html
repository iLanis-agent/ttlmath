TTL Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out DNS cache timing from the numbers you type. It has not looked at your domain, your DNS provider or your records. It does not know which resolvers, browsers, operating systems or apps along the way ignore, stretch or cut a TTL, and it gives no verdict that a change will go smoothly. Every number is your own entry.

The wait truth

Before moving a record, lower its TTL and wait. TTLs are in seconds.

The load truth

What a TTL costs your server in questions from caches.

The missing name truth

How long a cache remembers that a name did not exist. Both numbers are on the SOA record.

TTL Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
